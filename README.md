# 🏫 Welcome to Hinthar International School Dev Team

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Hinthar International School**'s development team. We are a dedicated group of developers, and students from IAL and IGCSE working collaboratively to build a modern, integrated digital ecosystem for our school.

Our mission is to streamline school operations and enhance the educational experience for students and staff through intuitive, high-performance software.

---

## 🚀 Our Core Projects

We are currently engineering three primary platforms:

1. **School Management System (SMS):** The digital backbone of Hinthar International, handling administrative tasks, student records, and day-to-day operations.
2. **Learning Application:** A dedicated, interactive platform designed to deliver structured study materials, track progress, and support student learning.
3. **School Website:** The public-facing portal representing our school's values, announcements, and key information.

*(Note: Pinned repositories for these projects can be found below!)*

## 🤝 Contribution Guidelines

This organization is currently restricted to internal team members of Hinthar International School. 

**For Team Members:**
* Ensure you are pulling the latest changes from the `main` or `dev` branches before starting new work.
* Prefix your commits with your role/task (e.g., `[SMS-FE] Added dashboard layout`, `[LEARN-BE] Configured database schemas`).
* All major pull requests must be reviewed by the **Backend Lead Architect** or the **Project Manager** before merging.
* Coordinate with the **UI/UX & QA** team before finalizing any major UI overhauls.
